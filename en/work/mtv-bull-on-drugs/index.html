Work / 07

MTV / BULL ON DRUGS

From a simple gesture to an explosion of color

IdentityCharacters3D

Sequence ↘

An MTV ident designed and animated by Lucas Casagrande for Bluna. The bull and bullfighter first appear in a spare composition against a flat background. A sweep of the cape opens a world of floating characters, repetition and saturated color that fills the screen around the brand.

Using contrast to drive the story: a restrained scene becomes a psychedelic universe in just ten seconds.
