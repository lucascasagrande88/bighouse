PANDORA IN RIO

FOOH, event IDs and an opening film for one visual universe.

FOOHIdentityOpening3D

FOOH ↘Event IDs ↘Styleframes ↘

Pandora in Rio brings together different scales of the same visual experience. Charms appear in recognizable Rio de Janeiro locations; identity films explore light, materials and motion; and a 3D opening introduces the event’s world. The case also includes a selection of styleframes from the visual development.

The object as a starting point: changing its scale, exploring its material qualities and turning it into an identity that moves between the city and its screens.
