LAMICELL / V22

From concept to motion

Direction3DProduct

Images ↘

Direction of a motion graphics film for Lamicell V22 protective horse boots, developed with a team from the concept board through the final render and sound design. CGI brings the viewer closer to the product through surface details, changes of scale and visualizations of its structure. Produced in Cinema 4D and Redshift.

Maintaining a coherent visual direction from concept to final render.
