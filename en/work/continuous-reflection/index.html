CONTINUOUS REFLE(A)CTION 2.0

Two ways back to nature

Art film3DNature

Factory ↘Flowers ↘

Two ten-second films created around the concept “Back to Nature” for Continuous Refle(a)ction 2.0, Morcreate Beijing’s virtual eco-art exhibition. Factory transforms a factory suspended among clouds into a structure of wind turbines and vegetation. Flowers approaches the same change at another scale: flowers spread across the ground, opening the image onto an urban landscape.

Telling a story of transformation in ten seconds. Industry and the city change through color, growth and camera movement.
