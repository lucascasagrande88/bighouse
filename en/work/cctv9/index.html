CCTV9

NOW / On-screen identity

BroadcastIdentityMotion

Images ↘

An identity system for CCTV9, China’s documentary channel. The published project includes a logo, lower thirds, a menu and continuity elements such as Next and Now, alongside explorations of a cube. Developed at JL Design in Shanghai, with Lucas as Head of Motion Design working with the team.

Building a precise brand presence through geometry, materials and negative space.
