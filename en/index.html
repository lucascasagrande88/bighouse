The journey behind the image

Craft before
tools.

Over fifteen years exploring how an idea becomes an image you can feel.

My path has taken me through studios, agencies, television and international projects in Argentina, Bahrain and Shanghai, including work at Memac Ogilvy, JL Design and MPC. Today, I connect art direction, motion, 3D and artificial intelligence from Buenos Aires.

Experimentation has always been part of the process: in my early reels, live visuals and personal work. AI adds tools to that exploration. The judgment to select, direct and finish a piece remains at the center.
