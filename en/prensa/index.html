Lucas Casagrande

Art direction,
motion and 3D.

I have worked in creative production for studios, agencies and television since 2009. My path connects Argentina, Bahrain and Shanghai, commercial projects and personal exploration.

I was an instructor at Domestika and founded CHIMICHURRI, where I bring together visual direction, production and artificial intelligence. My focus is on making images with identity and building processes that carry them through to completion.

My work Eternal Loop is part of the official Pause Originals archive. In 2020, I contributed two films to Morcreate’s online eco-art exhibition Continuous Refle(a)ction 2.0.

Information for press and teams

A little more context.

Who is Lucas Casagrande?

I am an art director, motion designer and 3D artist. I have worked in creative production since 2009, across studios, agencies and television in Argentina and on international projects. I am now based in Buenos Aires and lead CHIMICHURRI.

What kind of projects does he work on?

I develop art direction, motion and 3D for brands and creative teams. The portfolio includes product films, motion identities, characters, FOOH and event visuals. It also brings together personal work and studies of form, materials and space.

Where can I download the press kit and artwork selection?

This page includes the press kit and artwork catalogue, each available as a landscape or portrait PDF. The press kit brings together a biography, a visual overview and conversation topics; the catalogue presents a selection from the artwork archive.

Does he offer talks and workshops for teams?

Yes. Topics include art direction, visual consistency in AI-assisted workflows and the journey from brief to finished image. The content and scope are adapted to each team or institution. Get in touch through the contact page to discuss a proposal.
