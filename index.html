Who I Am
ABOUT
Lucas

Lucas Casagrande is a Creative Director and Motion Designer with more than 15 years turning ideas into moving images. His work lives where art direction, 3D and storytelling meet — from photo-real CGI to the raw energy of a live show.

He has directed motion and CGI for global names like Toyota, Pandora, Gillette and Snapdragon, working with studios and agencies including MPC Shanghai, Ogilvy Bahrain and Superunion — pieces that have been exhibited across three continents.

For three years he was the visual director of the entire stage production at Creamfields, running the live visuals behind some of the biggest DJs in the world — thousands of people, one screen, zero second takes.

Beyond client work, Lucas has lectured at the UBA and the UAI and exhibited at the Centro Cultural Recoleta (now El Recoleta) as part of Ciudad Emergente. Lately he's been pushing the intersection of creativity and emerging technology, folding AI into his production pipeline and teaching it through his courses on Domestika.
